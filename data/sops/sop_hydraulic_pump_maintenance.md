# Standard Operating Procedure: Hydraulic Power Unit (HPU) Overhaul & Valve Servicing
## SOP Reference: SOP-MNT-HYD-008 (Rev 2.1)
**Applicable Equipment**: TitanPress-3000, Hydropress-1200, Extruder-E40
**Department**: Fluid Power & Hydraulic Services

### 1. Safety Procedures
- Depressurize system completely. Verify high-pressure gauge reads 0 bar before loosening any fitting or flange.
- Lock out electrical power to 160 kW main drive motor.
- Place containment drip trays (min 200L capacity) beneath pump manifold.

### 2. Required Spares & Equipment
- Proportional Valve Replacement Cartridge: Rexroth 4WRPEH 6-C
- High-Pressure Filter Element: 3-micron absolute micro-glass (Part: Rexroth 2.0040 H3XL-A00-0-M)
- Portable Fluid Transfer / Off-Line Filtration Cart (Target cleanliness: ISO 15/13/10)
- Fluke 773 Process Milliamp Clamp Meter for 4-20mA valve loop diagnostics.

### 3. Execution Steps

#### Step 1: Fluid Sampling & Pre-Check
1. Connect sampling adapter to sample port MP-03.
2. Run fluid cart for 5 minutes; extract 100 mL into clean particle sample bottle.
3. Test with on-site laser particle counter. If NAS 1638 class is >7, activate polishing bypass filtration for 4 hours.

#### Step 2: Main Pump Inspection & Swashplate Servo Adjustment
1. Inspect swashplate cradle bearings for fretting through the inspection port.
2. Measure case drain line temperature with infrared pyrometer (must be within 8°C of reservoir bulk temperature).
3. Check pilot control pressure: adjust pilot pressure relief valve PRV-02 to 4.5 MPa +/- 0.2 MPa.

#### Step 3: High Pressure Filter Replacement
1. Unscrew filter bowl using filter strap wrench.
2. Inspect bottom of bowl for metallic slivers or bronze flakes (indicates pump piston shoe wear).
3. Coat new O-ring seal with clean hydraulic oil; install filter element (torque bowl to 70 Nm).
4. Reset red differential pressure pop-up visual indicator.

#### Step 4: System Air Bleeding & Recommissioning
1. Open manual air bleed screws on highest point of main ram cylinder manifold.
2. Jog main pump in 2-second bursts at 300 RPM until oil emerges from bleed ports without air bubbles or foaming.
3. Tighten bleed screws to 25 Nm.
4. Run press under no-load pressure cycle at 5 MPa for 15 minutes while monitoring sound level (<78 dBA at 1 meter).
